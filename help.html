Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a queue? ›
Open College Services, choose the service you need, and click the Join Queue button. Your queue number will be generated automatically.
Where can I see my queue position? ›
Open My Queue from the sidebar. You can see your queue number, position, people ahead of you and estimated waiting time.
Can I leave a queue? ›
Yes. Open My Queue and select the queue you want to leave. Click Leave Queue to remove yourself from that active queue.
Can I join more than one queue? ›
Yes. QueueLess allows you to join different college services at the same time, as long as you do not already have an active queue for the same service.
How will I know when my turn is near? ›
QueueLess continuously updates your queue position. Notifications can be shown when your turn is approaching.
What happens when my turn arrives? ›
The Queue Manager calls the next waiting student. Your queue status changes as the queue progresses.
How can I give feedback? ›
Open Feedback from the sidebar. Select a star rating, write your comments and click Submit Feedback.
What should I do if something is not working? ›
First refresh the page and make sure you are logged in. If the problem continues, contact the QueueLess support team using the support options below.
